Welcome

This helps you pick colors and fonts for a simple app screen. You make a few choices on the left. The fake app on the right updates as you go.

Your job: click Start, then keep clicking Continue until you can download a CSS file.

We already picked good defaults (cream page, soft orange bar — like the plgkit demo). You only need to change things if you want a different look.

Good habit Design a real piece of the app first (here: the white announcement card), not empty layout shells.

Step 1 — What should it feel like?

Tap one button. It sets fonts and how round the corners are.

Your job: pick one option, then click Continue.
Good habit Fancy serif titles feel “classic.” Plain system fonts feel “app-like.” Neither is wrong — pick a mood.

Step 2 — Pick your main color

This is the color of the top bar. Click the big color square and choose something you like. We figure out the page background, text, and borders for you.

Your job: click the color square (or skip if you like what you see).

In your book download, open the folder Color Palettes JSON and choose any file like palette-01.json. It never leaves your computer.

Good habit You need more than one swatch in a real app. Either use a palette file from the book, or let this tool fill in the rest from your one main color.

Step 3 — Text sizes

Most people can leave this alone and just hit Continue.

Body text starts at 16 pixels. Headlines grow from that in fixed steps so you don’t end up with 47 random font sizes.

Good habit Pick sizes from a short list. If every line needs a new size, the page will look messy.

Step 4 — Gaps and padding

Default is fine — click Continue unless you want tighter layouts.

Padding and gaps snap to a simple grid (8 pixels by default) so spacing looks even.

Good habit When in doubt, leave more empty space first. You can always tighten later.

Step 5 — Take it to your project

Click Download CSS. You get a file named tokens.css. Open your project’s main stylesheet (often styles.css) and paste everything in that file at the top.

Your job: download, paste into CSS, reload your app.
Good habit Make the important thing stand out by keeping everything else quiet — one main button per card.
